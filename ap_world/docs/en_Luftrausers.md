## What is the Goal of this Randomizer?
The current goal choices are:
- Find the 3 CLASSIFIED parts and launch to URauser
- Gain a "Completion" with a configurable number of unique plane builds

A completion can currently be either a blimp kill or a configurable high score

## Where is the options page?
The options are included with the apworld and the yaml on the releases page, and include:

- Configurable goals
- Death Link (with a set number of deaths before it triggers)
- Randomizable missions
- Randomizable starting loadout
- Optional mission locking, behind Rank Keys shuffled into the pool
- A selection between generic completions (ie. gain x completions with any planes), or unique completons (ie. the randomizer will choose x planes that must gain completions)
- Starting health scaling
- Number and strength of powerups shuffled into the pool
- SFML mode for those who are godly at the game

## What does randomization do for this game?

Your plane parts are shuffled into the multiworld. Gaining rauser levels, completing missions, and getting configurable plane completions will let you send other players items. 

All 5 missions for every part (minus the CLASSIFIED URauser parts) are available in order. Each mission is a check, and each part is shuffled into the multiworld. Optionally Rank Keys can be shuffled that are required before completing later missions. 

Major game changes:
- All plane parts (except for your starting build) are shuffled into the multiworld
- All missions are available to do for unlocked parts in order, optionally locked by Rank Keys shuffled into the pool
- SFMT is an optional setting for those so inclined
- A Completions system where getting a blimp kill or a high score with unique planes is a check (or goal)
- Powerups can be shuffled into the pool to increase health, fire rate, and number of bullets fired

## What items and locations get shuffled?

Items:
- All plane parts
- Optional Rank Keys
- Powerups
- Filler in the form of Patch Ups and various traps

Locations:
- Mission completions
- Rauser levels
- "Completions" with each plane (to a set maximum)

## What items can be in another player's world?
Any item above

## What does another world's items look like in Luftrausers?
Available missions will hint the item behind them, and show what's sent in the UI when completed. Currently rauser levels and completions don't have UI elements associated with them (but it's planned!)

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
Parts will unlock and powerups will be applied. Currently no UI elements show on item receive (but it's also planned!)
